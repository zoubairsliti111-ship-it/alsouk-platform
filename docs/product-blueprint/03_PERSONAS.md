**Author:** Chief Product Officer & Lead UX Architect

---

## 1. Persona Mapping Overview

To build a platform that matches regional trading habits, we define 10 highly specific personas representing the B2B ecosystem of Tunisia and surrounding North African markets.

---

## 2. Supplier Personas

### 2.1 The Manufacturer
*   **Name:** Slimane Ben Salem (52)
*   **Role:** Factory Owner / Manufacturer
*   **Business:** "Carthage Plastics" (Sousse, Tunisia) - Custom injection molding and agricultural packaging.
*   **Goals:** Secure high-volume manufacturing contracts from national agricultural cooperatives and exporters; showcase factory machinery and production capacities.
*   **Pain Points:** Traditional catalogs get outdated; global buyers can't see the factory's scale; high cost of attending physical trade fairs.
*   **Daily Workflow:** Spends 70% of his day on the factory floor supervising production; checks phone during breaks; reviews pricing and raw materials costs in the evening.
*   **Mobile Usage:** 95% mobile usage. Frequently shoots short video clips of active machines to send to clients via WhatsApp.
*   **Technical Knowledge:** Moderate. Uses WhatsApp, Facebook Pages, and YouTube. Struggles with complex ERPs or database interfaces.
*   **Needs:** A simple way to publish short videos showcasing active manufacturing machinery and link them directly to products.

### 2.2 The Wholesaler
*   **Name:** Chokri El Abed (45)
*   **Role:** Wholesale Distributor
*   **Business:** "El Abed Grossiste" (Rue d'Espagne, Tunis) - Bulk cosmetics and personal care products.
*   **Goals:** Liquidate high-volume inventory quickly to maintain cash flow; find retail buyers across different cities (Sfax, Gabes, Bizerte).
*   **Pain Points:** High competition in local commercial districts; retail buyers waste time haggling over low quantities; catalog prices fluctuate based on currency.
*   **Daily Workflow:** Manages active stock in a crowded physical shop; coordinates with delivery van drivers; receives continuous walk-in retail clients.
*   **Mobile Usage:** 100% mobile. Keeps phone in hand to answer incoming WhatsApp queries and coordinate delivery routes.
*   **Technical Knowledge:** High social-media literacy. Constantly posts active stock on Facebook/Instagram stories, but has no official website.
*   **Needs:** A mobile-first storefront where they can list daily stock levels and set Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ) to filter out individual consumer queries.

### 2.3 The Importer
*   **Name:** Amel Bouazizi (38)
*   **Role:** Import Director
*   **Business:** "Sourcing Nord-Afrique" (La Goulette, Tunis) - Importer of industrial textiles and machinery parts from Turkey and China.
*   **Goals:** Connect with local manufacturing factories and wholesale distributors needing raw fabrics; display incoming shipments in advance.
*   **Pain Points:** Bureaucratic port customs delays; pricing changes rapidly with international freight costs; tracking client queries across separate platforms is chaotic.
*   **Daily Workflow:** Splits time between the port, customs offices, and her main office; negotiates with Turkish factories via WeChat and local clients via WhatsApp.
*   **Mobile Usage:** 85% mobile. Relies on mobile messaging apps to coordinate logistics and forward customs documents.
*   **Technical Knowledge:** High. Comfortable using diverse digital sourcing systems, importing tools, and messaging channels.
*   **Needs:** A verified corporate profile that displays her import licenses, active certificates, and digital presence links in a professional layout.

### 2.4 The Exporter
*   **Name:** Hedi Chaibi (47)
*   **Role:** Agricultural Exporter
*   **Business:** "Chaibi Deglet Nour" (Tozeur / Tunis) - Premium Tunisian dates exporter to European and Libyan markets.
*   **Goals:** Find international B2B buyers looking for certified organic dates; manage seasonal crop orders.
*   **Pain Points:** High barrier to proving international quality standards (ISO, Bio certifications); European buyers demand rigorous company profile audits.
*   **Daily Workflow:** Inspects date plantations in southern Tunisia; coordinates packaging and temperature-controlled logistics; manages export customs filings.
*   **Mobile Usage:** 90% mobile. Uses phone to photograph sorting and packaging lines to verify quality to buyers.
*   **Technical Knowledge:** Moderate. Uses emails for formal European negotiations, but prefers messaging for regional traders.
*   **Needs:** A dedicated certificate gallery where he can upload ISO and organic certifications that display as prominent trust badges.

### 2.5 The Distributor
*   **Name:** Moncef Mezghani (34)
*   **Role:** Regional Logistics Distributor
*   **Business:** "Mezghani Distribution" (Sfax, Tunisia) - Food and beverage regional distributor.
*   **Goals:** Secure long-term supply agreements with local juice and pasta factories; distribute efficiently to small retailers.
*   **Pain Points:** High transport fuel costs; empty return-trips; coordination delays between factory production and distributor transport.
*   **Daily Workflow:** Manages warehouse loading docks; monitors delivery vans; negotiates pricing with factory owners and retail networks.
*   **Mobile Usage:** 90% mobile. Keeps logistics routes and order spreadsheets open on phone.
*   **Technical Knowledge:** Good. Uses maps, GPS tracking apps, and WhatsApp business.
*   **Needs:** Clear, localized search directory filtered by City and Business Type to identify local manufacturers with ready inventory.

## 3. Buyer Personas

### 3.1 The Retailer (Local Shop Owner)
*   **Name:** Leila Mansour (29)
*   **Role:** Boutique Owner
*   **Business:** "Leila Chic" (Bizerte, Tunisia) - Retail clothing store.
*   **Goals:** Find reliable Tunisian wholesale manufacturers supplying trending local clothing lines; secure small B2B quantities (MOQ of 50-100 items).
*   **Pain Points:** Travelling to Tunis weekly to source inventory takes a whole day and costs transport; wholesale pricing transparency is non-existent.
*   **Daily Workflow:** Runs her physical boutique; manages retail clients; packs online orders; sources inventory online late at night.
*   **Mobile Usage:** 100% mobile. Uses phone to record boutique TikTok videos and source inventory during quiet shop hours.
*   **Technical Knowledge:** Very high consumer digital literacy. Navigates Instagram, TikTok, and WhatsApp seamlessly. Distrusts complex desktop web portals.
*   **Needs:** A visual, snapping product feed with direct WhatsApp negotiation options, allowing her to source new inventory without leaving her shop.

### 3.2 The Sourcing Procurement Manager
*   **Name:** Karim Daoud (42)
*   **Role:** Corporate Procurement Manager
*   **Business:** "Tunis-Lait Group" (Tunis) - Major regional food processing enterprise.
*   **Goals:** Locate high-capacity local manufacturers of custom food packaging; secure competitive bulk rates.
*   **Pain Points:** Finding verified local factories is slow; paper RFQs take weeks to coordinate; suppliers lack digital catalogs with accurate MOQs.
*   **Daily Workflow:** Reviews corporate procurement requests; meets with suppliers; manages formal bidding processes in his office.
*   **Mobile Usage:** 50% mobile, 50% desktop. Uses desktop for spreadsheets and formal filings; uses mobile for messaging.
*   **Technical Knowledge:** High. Comfortable with enterprise ERPs and procurement software.
*   **Needs:** A clean, multi-step **RFQ engine** that allows him to submit technical sourcing requests and compare verified supplier bids.

## 4. Internal Business Roles

### 4.1 The Sales Employee
*   **Name:** Faten Gharbi (26)
*   **Role:** Sales & Marketing Assistant
*   **Employer:** "Carthage Plastics" (Slimane's manufacturing company)
*   **Goals:** Manage incoming digital inquiries; keep the company's product catalog and active stock details up to date.
*   **Pain Points:** The business owner (Slimane) frequently changes pricing and stock levels but forgets to communicate them; managing client contacts via Slimane's personal WhatsApp is difficult.
*   **Daily Workflow:** Answers customer phone calls; uploads product photos; responds to social media messages; updates basic stock details.
*   **Mobile Usage:** 80% mobile, 20% desktop.
*   **Technical Knowledge:** High. Familiar with Canva, CapCut, social media management, and online forms.
*   **Needs:** Multi-user permission settings where the owner can grant her edit access to the storefront catalog without sharing owner account settings.

### 4.2 The Company Owner (Decoupled Entity Owner)
*   **Name:** Slimane Ben Salem (52) *(Same as 2.1)*
*   **Role:** Ultimate Company Owner
*   **Goals:** Delegate day-to-day digital storefront updates and message management to his Sales Assistant (Faten) while maintaining full control over company parameters and bank integrations.
*   **Pain Points:** Fear of losing control of his digital assets; lack of time to update catalogs; security risks of sharing passwords with temporary staff.
*   **Daily Workflow:** Oversees factory floor; signs financial contracts; reviews sales reports.
*   **Needs:** Decoupled owner role inside the `company_members` join table, allowing him to revoke employee access with one tap.

## 5. Administration Personas

### 5.1 The Platform Admin
*   **Name:** Youssef Toumi (31)
*   **Role:** Lead Admin & Sourcing Controller
*   **Employer:** "ALSOUK Operations"
*   **Goals:** Review and verify submitted merchant profiles; audit tax documents against the RNE registry; moderate public RFQ submissions to block spam.
*   **Pain Points:** High volume of daily submissions; incomplete company records; manually checking Tunisian tax IDs (Matricule Fiscal) takes time.
*   **Daily Workflow:** Reviews the admin dashboard; inspects pending companies; approves verified badges; routes active RFQs to matching categories.
*   **Mobile Usage:** 30% mobile, 70% desktop. Uses a desktop-optimized admin panel to manage databases.
*   **Technical Knowledge:** Very High. Expert in database query inspection, moderation protocols, and service client tools.
*   **Needs:** A central, protected **Admin Sourcing Controller** to view, filter, and approve companies, certificates, and incoming buyer RFQs.
